Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CleanMyX processes information when you connect an X account and use the app.

Information we process

  • X account access: information needed to connect your X account, retrieve posts and liked posts, delete the posts you select, and remove the likes you select.
  • Account details: X account ID, screen name, and profile details.
  • Like removal and deletion activity: selected post IDs, batch status, counts, timestamps, and error status needed to operate and support deletion and like removal.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate your account, display available posts and liked posts, and perform deletions you request.

Service providers

We use X to access account content. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Children

CleanMyX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact
